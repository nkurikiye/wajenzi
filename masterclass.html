The masterclass

One day to decode what makes a company grow.

An interactive masterclass on entrepreneurship, innovation, private equity and wealth creation. Starting from the journeys of entrepreneurs who built great companies, participants decode the choices that make companies grow, experience how venture capital works and build their own growth strategy.

The journey

Six steps, one question: what to build?

1

Opportunity

What must you build so it works without you?

2

Innovation

Zero to one: creating what did not exist.

3

Scalable model

Can it repeat, without rebuilding everything?

4

Capital

The right capital, at the right stage: bootstrapping, debt, private equity (PE) and venture capital (VC), including the VC power law.

5

Growth

Session: scaling up your business beyond borders.

6

Wealth

Build, grow, protect: ownership, dilution, liquidity, diversification.

The format is interactive, with team workshops: the builders’ lab and the decision room. A cross-border view: Africa · Luxembourg · the Netherlands.

The day

One day, two parts.

Who is it for? Entrepreneurs and business owners, startup founders, investors and professionals who support Africa’s economic transformation.

09:00 – 12:00

Masterclass

The six-step journey, with case studies of entrepreneurs who built great companies.

13:00 – 15:00

Business clinic

Small-group working session on your own business.
