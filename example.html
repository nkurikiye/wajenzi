What a €5M raise looks like.

A hypothetical scenario, built only to show how a typical raise comes together — from a profitable company in Africa to a bond that European and diaspora investors can confidently fund. It does not describe a real transaction.

Instrument

Listing

Security
Charge over processing assets & receivables
