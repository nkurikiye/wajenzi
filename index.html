The challenge

Good companies can be difficult to assess from a distance.

Growth-stage African businesses often have fragmented financial and operational information. Investors need clear, comparable evidence to assess an opportunity. Wajenzi brings information together to help companies prepare and investors review opportunities more efficiently.

What we do

We help turn company information into a clearer path to capital.

01

Assess investment readiness

We help companies organise key business, financial and governance information, identify gaps and understand what investors may need to review.

02

Explore financing routes

We assess which capital structures may suit a company's needs, including debt, bonds or pooled vehicles — subject to feasibility and the involvement of appropriate partners.

03

Identify relevant investor interest

We use AI-supported analysis to identify potential matches based on stated criteria such as sector, geography, investment size and impact priorities. Our team reviews the results before introductions.

How we use AI

AI assists the work. People review the decisions.

We use AI-supported tools to organise company information, identify investment-readiness gaps and surface potential investor matches. We make the basis for a potential match clear: the criteria considered, the available evidence and the information still needed.

AI supports assessment and discovery. It does not guarantee funding, replace due diligence or make investment decisions.
